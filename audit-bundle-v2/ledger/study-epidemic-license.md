# Study: Epidemic Sound licence — "Sunflower Dance" hackathon video

Verdicts: (i) PARTLY · (ii) PARTLY. Web research only (epidemicsound.com + help
centre). Not legal advice. Full per-question evidence:
[report.md](../../scratch/study-epidemic-license/report.md).

## Complete

- Q1: Pro covers online video incl. "websites"; monetise up to 3 channels/platform;
  safelist channels (incl. Website-URL field) or individual video URLs; content
  published while active stays cleared forever.
- Q2: Pro carries full sublicensing rights; third-party publication is cleared by
  adding the video URL ("Safelist a Video") or a 30-day clearing invitation (active
  subscription required at clearing time).
- Q3: Attribution is recommended how-to ("Artist / Title / courtesy of
  www.epidemicsound.com"), not a licence condition on any fetched page.
- Q4: Cutting/looping to fit is allowed (trim/fade/time-fit within this); standalone
  use is banned — raw .mp3 must NOT be in the public repo; muxed video is the covered
  form. No remixes/covers distribution, no Content ID registration of edits.
- Q5: Published-while-active = cleared forever; publish after expiry = not cleared.
  Content ID claim on YouTube means no confirmed valid licence — dispute via YouTube
  Studio within 5 days with safelist + subscription details.
- Q6: "Sunflower Dance" by Helmut Schenker; 3:03 (183000 ms); 124 BPM; Adventure /
  Cinematic / Contemporary Classical; Dreamy, Happy; released 2024-09-30; no per-track
  restrictions on the page.
