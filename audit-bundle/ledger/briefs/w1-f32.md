**Mandated kernel shape (all ops):** decode with `src/msf_decode.h`
(`msf_decode`, exact sig×2^exp) → NaN delivery via
`msf_nan_delivery` (header records the SF-0 §2.5 reading; FMA order
= multiplicand, multiplier, addend) → special finite cases → exact
integer computation over `msf_u128` (`src/msf_wide.c`) → ONE
rounding via `msf_pack_f32` (cross-format dest: `msf_pack_generic`
is for your dest only — you own dest=32; conversions to f64 are the
other parcel's). No operation rounds twice; no `float`/`double`
types anywhere (the type scan refuses the words).
- add/sub: jam-align smaller significand to the larger exponent
  (`msf_u128_shr_jam`), add/subtract magnitudes by effective signs,
  pack once. Exact-zero rules per Clause 6.3 (x+(−x) is +0 except
  roundTowardNegative; −0 + −0 = −0).
- mul: `msf_u128_mul_u64_u64` (24×24 fits; 64-bit sources widen exactly); 0×∞ invalid via delivery
  helper with invalid_without_nan.
- div: exact long division producing ≥ p+2 quotient bits with the
  remainder as sticky evidence (binary shift/subtract over u128 is
  acceptable; correctness first). Division-by-zero → §7.3.
- sqrt: integer sqrt at p+2 bits with exact remainder → sticky;
  negative (incl. −∞) invalid; sqrt(−0) = −0.
- fma: exact product (48 bits for f32×f32; wider when a source is f64), addend jam-aligned per WIDTHS.md,
  single pack; 0×∞ + quiet-NaN addend signals invalid and delivers
  the addend (caller raises invalid, helper delivers).
- remainder: exact x − n·y, n the ties-to-even integer (scaled
  long division); exact result, sign of x; invalid only for x=∞ or
  y=0 (or NaN rules).
- roundToIntegral*: round in the significand domain; named forms
  signal no inexact; the Exact form signals inexact exactly when a
  nonzero fraction was discarded. sNaN via helper.
- nextUp/nextDown: adjacent encoding step (no flags except sNaN
  invalid); through ±0 to the smallest subnormal; maxFinite → ∞.
- scaleB: second operand is binary64 (logBFormat). Non-integral N
  is truncated toward zero (project ruling; 5.3.3 leaves it
  language-defined); huge |N| may clamp internally since results
  saturate, but flags must come out of the pack. scaleB(±0,N)=±0,
  scaleB(±∞,N)=±∞, scaleB(x,0)=x (NaN rules first).
- logB: normalized exponent (subnormals count their leading zeros),
  delivered as binary64 (logBFormat) even from your f32 source; logB(±0) = −∞ + divisionByZero;
  logB(±∞) = +∞.
- convertFormat (from f32, from f64; both land in your dest): single rounding through pack;
  NaN via helper (sNaN invalid); signed zeros preserved.
- from_int (8 widths): exact magnitude → pack.
- to_int (10 variants × 8 widths): project invalid rule in the
  header (saturate; NaN → 0) + invalid flag; inexact ONLY in the
  Exact variants. Beware the ties cases at integer boundaries.
- comparisons: 22 predicates from the four relations via
  `msf_cmp_finite` (exact, cross-format); signaling forms raise
  invalid on any NaN, quiet forms only on signaling NaN (§5.11).
- totalOrder/totalOrderMag: encoding-order keys within binary64.
