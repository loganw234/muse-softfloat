(3) ROM accuracy per datasheet 2.8.3.2.1: five basic ops correctly rounded in RN-even only, but input denormals treated as zero, input NaNs as infinities, output denormals flushed, output NaNs rendered as infinities, no traps and no flags anywhere; scientific functions within 1 ULP with named weak spots and +/-128 (float) / +/-1024 (double) trig ranges. (4) SIO per core: one 8-cycle 32/32 divider with DIRTY/READY save-restore contract and two interpolators (1-cycle shift/mask/sign-extend/add-base, POP writeback, INTERP0 blend, INTERP1 clamp) whose SDK claim byte is global, not per-core, with 7-word manual save/restore. (5) No FP state exists today; per-core homes that do exist are TLS default per_thread (.tls0/.tls1), arrays indexed by get_core_num (SDK precedent hw_divider_results[NUM_CORES]), and per-core SIO blocks; watchdog SCRATCH0-7 and spinlocks are shared, not per-core.
