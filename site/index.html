muse-softfloat

An IEEE 754 softfloat library for the RP2040 — binary16, binary32 and binary64 — with no floating-point unit underneath it. Every result is worked out in exact integer arithmetic and rounded once, correctly, in your choice of five rounding modes.

On silicon

Three workloads run bit-identically on a Raspberry Pi Pico (RP2040) through this library. The figures below are recorded facts from docs/RECORD.md, with the board protocol in docs/BOARD.md — quoted with their units and context, not as speed claims.

IAS15 — REBOUND's integrator on the Pico

What runs
REBOUND's IAS15 gravitational integrator (GPL, scratch only — it never enters this repository), its binary64 arithmetic served by this library over the AEABI compat surface.
Agreement
2,001 of 2,001 per-step state hashes match the unmodified desktop build exactly (step-2000 state hash begins 4d4530a6).
Recorded rate
1.58 accepted steps per second at 125 MHz, measured over a 1,000-step block. A full figure-8 period takes about ten minutes at that rate.

The sketch is an illustration of the figure-8 three-body choreography, drawn by this page. It is not simulation output and carries no data.

Interval geometry

What runs
Guaranteed ray classification (sphere, box, torus) over directed-rounded intervals, against a frozen 710-row exact-oracle catalogue.
Agreement
The full catalogue streamed to the board matches the host bit-for-bit: 710 of 710 rows, stream hash beginning ce81d66e on both sides; 7.92 s of workload-call time at 125 MHz.

KissFFT with runtime twiddles

What runs
KissFFT 131.2.0 (BSD-3, scratch only) with one named change: its setup phases hand exact cycle fractions to this library's sinPi/cosPi instead of calling host trigonometry. It is never described as unmodified KissFFT.
Agreement
All 12 configuration hashes bit-identical between host and board at the gated sizes N = 256/1024/4096 (complex and real, forward and inverse).

How this was verified

  • Named authorities, never self-consistency. Correctness is decided against Berkeley SoftFloat/TestFloat 3e (pinned by archive SHA-256 in docs/metrics/fetches.jsonl, executed in scratch, never vendored), MPFR, or an exact-integer reference — per instantiation, in docs/COVERAGE.tsv. The plans of record are the SF-0/SF-1/SF-2 studies in docs/studies/.
  • Planted copies. Independent verifiers re-derive each parcel's evidence against a tree carrying pre-ledgered defects; the score to date is in the table above, misses included. A gate that has not been watched to fail is not treated as a gate.
  • Board hashes. Silicon results name their firmware hash, commit and board, or they are not evidence; the three workloads above carry theirs into RECORD.md.
  • This page. The WebAssembly build is proven at build time by replaying the bundled sample of committed vectors through it under Node (site/replay.mjs, log in site/replay-log.txt); the count is in the table above. If WebAssembly is unavailable in your browser, the instrument says so and dealt vectors show their recorded results, labelled as recorded.

Status and what is still in progress

Tier 1 (the full Clause 5 surface) and the authorised Tier 2 prefix are landed and gated. Wave 5 has landed roundTiesToAway as the fifth rounding attribute and correctly rounded rSqrt and hypot at all three formats. Still in progress, and labelled as such wherever they appear: the exp2/log2 pairs and Clause 9.5 augmented arithmetic. Nothing beyond the SF-2 study's list is claimed.
