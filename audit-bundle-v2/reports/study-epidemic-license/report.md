# Study: Epidemic Sound licence facts for "Sunflower Dance" hackathon video

Scope: web research only, epidemicsound.com pages + help centre first.
No legal advice: this reports what the fetched terms say, per clause, with quotes and URLs.
Anything not groundable in a fetched page is marked unresolved.

## Verdicts on the owner's two clauses

- (i) "use here is valid under my Pro subscription" — **PARTLY**.
  The muxed-video sync use (music bed of an online demo video, trimmed/faded to fit,
  published during an active Pro subscription) is inside the Pro grant.
  Two gaps keep it from CONFIRMED: no fetched page names GitHub file hosting or a
  hackathon-submission platform as a covered/safel TLC-listable venue (closest text is
  "Publish anywhere online" / "websites" and the Website-URL safelist field), and the
  licence requires publication during an active subscription plus safelisting — neither
  is a fact about the world yet.
- (ii) "if Meta intends to publish the video it would require my disclosure to Epidemic
  but would otherwise be allowed" — **PARTLY**.
  The "disclosure" half is confirmed: Pro sublicensing is exercised by adding the
  published video's URL in the subscriber's account ("Safelist a Video") or a clearing
  invitation. The "otherwise allowed" half is not fully grounded: no page addresses
  contest-organiser showcase republication explicitly, and Meta-scale companies
  (>$10M turnover) may not themselves hold Pro — the sublicence covers content the
  subscriber produces for others' channels, but a hackathon repost is not textbook
  "client work". Only Epidemic support can settle it.

## Q1 — What the Pro plan covers for online video

- Venues. Pro "covers content for online platforms like YouTube, Facebook, Instagram,
  TikTok\*, Twitch, podcasting platforms, and websites."
  — [Pro Plan help article](https://www.epidemicsoundhelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/26247236323858-Pro-Plan)
- Marketing copy says "Publish anywhere online" and "Soundtracking with Epidemic Sound
  means you can publish content safely anywhere online, including the biggest platforms
  out there – as well as websites, games, apps, podcasts and more."
  — [Pro plan page](https://www.epidemicsound.com/our-plans/pro-plan/),
  [Our plans](https://www.epidemicsound.com/our-plans/)
- Channel limits / safelisting. "You can monetize up to 3 channels per platform"
  (Pro). "To be able to safelist your channel(s) and content, you need to have an
  active subscription." Safelist fields accept per-platform channel IDs plus a
  "Website URL: Full website URL" field, and individual videos can be safelisted by URL.
  — [Pro Plan help article](https://www.epidemicsoundhelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/26247236323858-Pro-Plan),
  [Safelisting](https://www.epidemicsoundhelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/26248340314258-Safelisting),
  [Safelist channels](https://www.epidemicsoundhelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/25890103714706-Safelist-channels)
- Monetisation. "What happens to content when a subscription is canceled? All content
  published while the subscription was active remains cleared to monetize permanently."
  — [Cancellation article](https://www.epidemicsoundhelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/26254650213266-What-happens-to-content-when-a-subscription-is-canceled)
- GitHub / hackathon platform. **Unresolved.** No fetched page names GitHub, git
  hosting, or hackathon/competition submission platforms. The grant's closest text is
  "anywhere online" / "websites". A muxed demo video is an audiovisual production
  (covered form); a video *file* sitting in a repo is not discussed anywhere.
  Safelisting is built around channel IDs/URLs and video URLs, so clearing works where
  claims are enforced (YouTube etc.); nothing says what safelisting a GitHub blob URL
  would do.

## Q2 — Third-party republication (Meta / organisers)

- Pro includes sublicensing: "Yes, you have full sublicensing rights, which means
  you're covered to produce content for your clients to publish on their channels."
  — [Pro plan page](https://www.epidemicsound.com/our-plans/pro-plan/)
- Mechanism ("disclosure" analogue): "If you produce content for others to publish on
  their channels and they want to avoid copyright claims, you simply add the URLs of
  the published video and we'll clear them for you."
  — [Our plans](https://www.epidemicsound.com/our-plans/)
- "There are two ways to clear client videos: 1. Clear the video link directly in your
  account. 2. Send an invitation to your client to clear the video themselves."
  "Note: An active subscription is required for your client to clear content using the
  invitation link."
  — [How to clear clients' videos](https://www.epidemicsoundhelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/26255832660370-How-to-clear-clients-videos)
- Limits: "The Pro Plan doesn't cover Video on Demand (VOD), Streaming VOD,
  Pay-per-View, virtual fitness classes, or local radio, TV shows, TV ads and cinema."
  "Additionally Publishers making over $5M/year and companies over $10M/year are not
  eligible for the Pro Plan."
  — [Pro Plan help article](https://www.epidemicsoundhelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/26247236323858-Pro-Plan)
- Assessment. The owner's clause (ii) correctly identifies the disclosure step (URL
  safelisting), but "otherwise allowed" goes beyond the text: (a) a Meta showcase
  repost is not shown to be "client work" / "content created for third parties";
  (b) Meta itself could not hold Pro at its scale, so its own-channel publication
  would rest entirely on the subscriber's sublicence + URL clearing, a combination no
  page blesses for non-client resharing. **Unresolved — ask Epidemic support**, giving
  them the exact republication facts.

## Q3 — Credits / attribution

- Not required by any fetched licence text. The help centre gives a how-to, not a
  must: "To properly credit Epidemic Sound, include the following in your videos,
  podcasts, or streams: Artist's Name / Title of Music Piece / courtesy of
  www.epidemicsound.com." Plus an optional share-link alternative. The same article
  adds: "Please note: A valid license or subscription is required when publishing your
  content to ensure continued monetization."
  — [How to credit](https://www.epidemicsoundhelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/26254804604562-How-to-credit-Epidemic-Sound-music-used-in-a-production)
- No fetched page conditions clearing/licence validity on crediting. Crediting is
  recommended practice, not a grant condition, on this evidence.

## Q4 — Edits; raw-track redistribution rule

- Edits to fit picture are permitted: "You can use both complete tracks and separate
  stems (bass, instrumental, vocals) as well as cut the music as you need for your
  content."
  — [Team subscription page (same licence wording family)](https://www.epidemicsound.com/our-plans/team-subscription/)
- "While tracks can be cut or looped to fit content, they cannot be shared as remixes
  or edits on platforms like Spotify, Apple Music, or SoundCloud."
  — [Covers/remixes article](https://www.epidemicsoundhelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/26254611350674-Can-covers-or-remixes-of-Epidemic-Sound-music-be-distributed)
- A ~2.7% time-compression / trim / fade to fit a ~3-minute video is within "cut or
  looped to fit content" on a plain reading (no page forbids tempo adjustment; no page
  explicitly blesses resampling either — minor residual gap, low risk).
- Standalone redistribution is prohibited: "Our music is intended to be used as
  background music for your video or podcast productions. … you can't use the music on
  a standalone basis, such as in audio samples, sound libraries, music beds, or via
  third parties."
  — [Our plans](https://www.epidemicsound.com/our-plans/)
- "No. Epidemic Sound does not permit the distribution of covers, remixes, or edited
  versions of its music. … Tracks or edits may not be registered in music recognition
  systems (e.g. Content ID). … Any use outside the scope of the license is considered
  copyright infringement."
  — [Covers/remixes article](https://www.epidemicsoundhelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/26254611350674-Can-covers-or-remixes-of-Epidemic-Sound-music-be-distributed)
- Verdict for the repo: the muxed demo video (music bed under original visuals) is the
  covered form. **The raw .mp3 must NOT sit in the public git repo** — that is
  standalone distribution ("sound libraries … or via third parties" / "standalone
  listening experiences"). Also barred: still-image "music video" uploads, compilations,
  lyric videos.
  — [Compilations article](https://www.epidemicsoundhelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/26254496789266-Can-Epidemic-Sound-music-be-used-for-compilations-or-music-listening-content)

## Q5 — Term; Content ID handling

- Term: "All videos published during an active subscription will be cleared forever."
  "Any videos published after the subscription period has ended will not be cleared."
  — [Pro plan page](https://www.epidemicsound.com/our-plans/pro-plan/)
- "All content published during a trial or active subscription will stay cleared
  forever." (Our-plans banner.)
  — [Our plans](https://www.epidemicsound.com/our-plans/)
- Content ID: "A Content ID claim from Epidemic Sound on a long-form video means
  YouTube has detected Epidemic Sound music but couldn't confirm the user has a valid
  license." Before disputing: "The correct YouTube channel is connected to the
  subscription or the video URL is linked to the license" and the content "is not
  published as a *listening experience*". Dispute via YouTube Studio with contact email
  + licence/subscription details; "Disputing within 5 days … ensures no loss of ad
  revenue if the claim is released." "Epidemic Sound reserves the right to monetize
  any use of its music that isn't properly licensed."
  — [Content ID claim article](https://www.epidemicsoundhelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/26253712691730-Why-was-a-Content-ID-claim-received-from-Epidemic-Sound)
- Expectation for the owner: publish + safelist while Pro is active; if YouTube flags
  the video, dispute with the safelisted channel/video and subscription details.
  Claims on Shorts "don't affect monetization … so there's normally nothing you need
  to do" (not this use, noted for completeness).

## Practical read-through (not legal advice)

- Publish the video while Pro is active; safelist the video URL/channel immediately.
- Ship only the muxed video in the public repo — never the .mp3 (or stems).
- Trim/fade/time-fit is fine; don't upload a still-image or music-only cut.
- Credit line (Artist / Title / courtesy of www.epidemicsound.com) is good practice,
  not a licence condition on current evidence.
- If Meta/organisers may repost: safelist their video URL(s) from the Pro account
  while active, keep proof of publication date, and confirm with Epidemic support
  since organiser resharing is not explicitly covered.
